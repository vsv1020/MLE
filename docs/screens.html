Study session · Features/Study/StudySessionView.swift

The rating bar shows what each button will actually do.

Tap Show answer, then rate the card. The four intervals are not illustrative — they are FSRS-5 output for this card's stored stability and difficulty, computed with the published default weights. Showing the consequence of every choice is what makes a scheduler feel trustworthy instead of arbitrary.

The prompt does not move when the answer appears, the reveal is the app's exact motion spec (0.3 s ease-out, opacity plus a 4 pt rise), and it collapses to a cross-fade when the viewer prefers reduced motion.
